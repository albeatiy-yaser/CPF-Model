The basics

What is a Central Processing Facility?

A Central Processing Facility (CPF) is the plant where the fluids from many oil wells are collected and processed. What comes out of a well is never pure oil. It is a mixture of oil, gas and water, sometimes with sand and salt.

The CPF separates this mixture and treats the oil until it meets the sales specification. The oil is then stored, measured and exported by pipeline or tanker. The gas and water go to their own systems for compression, re-injection or disposal.

Typical export crude specification

Water content (BS&W)≤ 0.5 %
Salt content≤ 10 PTB
Vapour pressure (RVP)≤ 10 – 12 psia
H₂SPer contract, e.g. < 10 – 60 ppm

Typical values only. Each export contract sets its own limits.
